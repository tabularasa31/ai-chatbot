## 2026-10-17 — New website and blog design

The Chat9 website and blog have a new design.

- They support light and dark themes, with a theme switch in the footer.
- The home page shows a live demo of the widget next to the sign-up button, and describes setup and handoff to your team step by step.
- Blog articles open as a single column, and the blog index is a list of articles.

**What to do:** nothing.

## 2026-10-16 — Redesigned sign-in pages

Sign in, create account, email verification, password reset and team invitation pages have been redesigned.

- They support light and dark themes, with a theme switch under the form.
- Errors are in plain words instead of technical messages.
- The password rule is shown when you create an account or choose a password, and is checked before you submit.
- An expired reset or invitation link offers **Request a new link**.
- There is no delay after signing in.

**What to do:** nothing. See [Getting started](/docs/getting-started) for creating an account.

## 2026-10-16 — New dashboard menu

The dashboard sidebar is regrouped into fewer items.

- **Install widget** replaces **Dashboard** and **Embed**: your bot ID, embed snippet and options are on one page.
- **Settings** is one menu item with four tabs: **Bot**, **Widget**, **Team** and **Seats**. These used to be separate items.
- **Doc gaps** is the new name of **Gap Analyzer**.
- **Knowledge** is the new name of **Knowledge Hub**.
- **Platform metrics** is the new name of **Admin** (visible to platform admins only).

Notices are now ranked by urgency. An OpenAI key problem (missing, rejected or out of credits) is shown at the top of every page; for owners the button goes to Settings → Bot or to your OpenAI billing. Product announcements appear as a quiet note on **Install widget**.

URLs are unchanged, and `/embed` now redirects to the **Install widget** page.

**What to do:** nothing. See [Dashboard Features](/docs/dashboard-features) for where each section lives.

## 2026-10-16 — One typeface across the product

The dashboard, website, sign-in pages and docs now use one typeface, Geist, instead of Arial or the browser's default serif.

**Chat widget:** the widget on your site now uses the visitor's system font and downloads no font file. Text may look slightly different from before.

**What to do:** nothing. Colours are unchanged; text may be spaced slightly differently.

See [Embedding the widget](/docs/embedding-the-widget) for widget setup.

## 2026-10-16 — Clearer errors in Settings, Inbox, Seats and embed snippet

Dashboard pages now say so when data fails to load, instead of showing empty or misleading values.

**Settings:** if part of the page fails to load, that section shows an error with a **Retry** button instead of empty fields. Saving can no longer replace your stored bot instructions, support inbox email, escalation language or response level with blanks. Each settings card shows its own save errors.

**Inbox:** the list no longer says nobody is waiting when it failed to load, and a failed refresh keeps the current list on screen and shows a Retry button. The last-message preview shows "You:" only for your own replies and the colleague's email for replies from others.

**Seats and the Dashboard embed snippet:** both show an error with a Retry button instead of "$0" or an empty snippet when their data fails to load.

**What to do:** nothing. Owners may want to confirm their bot instructions and support inbox email in Settings.

See [Dashboard features](/docs/dashboard-features) for the Settings and Inbox pages.

## 2026-09-29 — Widget identity verification (optional)

You can now verify that a `user_id` passed to the widget really belongs to the visitor.

**What's new:** generate a signing key in **Dashboard → Widget settings → Widget identity verification** (now Settings → Widget). Your server computes a `user_hash` from the key and the visitor's `user_id`, and passes it alongside `user_id` in `userHints`.

**Why you might want it:** without a key, user IDs are trusted as-is, so anyone who knows or guesses a visitor's ID can open their conversation. With a key, only visitors with a valid `user_hash` restore conversations across devices and are grouped in the Inbox; unsigned IDs only personalize the chat and keep it in the same browser.

See [Embedding the Widget → Identity verification](/docs/embedding-the-widget#identity-verification) for the full setup guide.
